Recommendation Engine Design

This should combine:

1️ Context-Based Logic
	Destination
	Time window
	Cruise duration
	Budget level

2️ Behavioral Data
	Previous bookings
	Search history
	Click behavior

3️ Popularity Signals
	Google ratings
	Review counts
	Trending searches
