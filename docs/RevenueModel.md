## 1. Platform Overview

The Tour Excursion Booking Platform is an AWS-based travel planning ecosystem that enables travelers to discover, plan, and book excursions, dining, shopping, and activities within a **unified itinerary planning experience**. The platform serves cruise passengers, vacation travelers, and group planners.

**Key capabilities:**

| Capability | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Shore excursion marketplace | Browse & book local excursions |
| AI-powered itinerary planning | Automated trip generation & optimization |
| Collaborative trip planning | Multi-user shared itinerary editing |
| Dining & shopping recommendations | Curated local business discovery |
| Dynamic pricing | Demand-based excursion pricing |
| Affiliate integrations | Partner revenue across dining, shopping & hotels |
| Vendor management tools | SaaS tools for excursion providers |

---

## 2. Core Revenue Stream — Shore Excursion Marketplace

The platform operates as a **marketplace** connecting excursion vendors with travelers.

**How it works:**

1. Vendors list excursions on the platform
2. Customers browse and book excursions
3. Platform earns a **commission on each booking**

**Commission structure:**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Average commission rate | 20–30% |
| Example: $200 excursion | $40–$60 platform revenue |

**Benefits for vendors:**

- Increased visibility to cruise passengers
- Intelligent scheduling based on port arrival times
- Higher conversion through integrated itinerary planning

## 3. Trip Planning Monetization

Trip planning is a **freemium collaboration platform** with tiered pricing.

### Free Tier
- 1 free trip plan
- Up to 3 travelers
- Basic itinerary builder
- Excursion booking
- View-only sharing

### Pro Trip Planner — *$8–$15 per trip*
- Unlimited edits
- Up to 10 travelers
- Shared editing
- Smart scheduling
- Destination recommendations
- Conflict detection

### Group Planner — *$25–$50 per trip*
- Up to 20+ participants
- Collaborative editing
- Activity voting
- Expense splitting
- Group chat

### Event / Cruise Planner — *$100–$300 per trip*
- Large groups (50–100+ participants)
- Centralized group management
- Group excursion booking
- Trip broadcasting

---

## 4. Collaboration-Based Pricing

Pricing scales with the **number of collaborators** editing the itinerary, encouraging viral adoption while monetizing complex coordination.

| Editors | Price |
|---------|-------|
| 1–3 editors | Free |
| 4–6 editors | $12 |
| 7–12 editors | $25 |
| 12+ editors | $50 |

> This model encourages viral adoption while monetizing complex trip coordination.

## 9. Dynamic Pricing Revenue

The platform captures additional margin during **high-demand periods**.

**Example:**

| | Price |
|-|-------|
| Base excursion price | $100 |
| Dynamic (peak demand) price | $120 |

**Revenue split:** Vendor receives base price; additional margin is shared between the vendor and the platform.
